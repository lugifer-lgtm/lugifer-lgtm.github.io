Well, we meet quickly!!! I am going to talk about something I visited a long time ago. So quite some time back, my professor asked me if I wanted to go an all-India conference for free and opensource apps. Like any sane human in his right senses, I said YES PLEASSEE!!!! I had a class on the first day so I went pretty late, I didn’t get to see any of the talks, but the stalls outside, where people presented and showcased their projects were amazing, I fell in love with each and every one of them, but to put together the really cool ones, there was a guy who made an adblocker, a guy who made a cute looking desk Ai buddy, I wanted to buy it, but it was out of my budget, and then there was this person who made a replica of jupyter labs. 2nd day had much better things, LINUX Line-up!!!! There was every linux on the 2nd floor. It was my dream right there; I took a brochure of everything. I met another first year dude who made a drone, and a social platform for developers, we exchanged contacts and he told me when his platform comes out. So yeah, that was indeed my favourite experience.
